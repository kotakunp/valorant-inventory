Valorant Loadout Studio

Share your loadout: your account’s cosmetics in one image

A free web tool that lets you view the cosmetics owned on your own VALORANT account — weapon skins and knives, player cards, player titles, and gun buddies — together with your account level, current and peak rank, and wallet balances, then exports everything as a single image or a share link you can send to friends or post in community spaces.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your own Riot session (or paste your session tokens — processed in memory only, never stored).
  2. Pick what to show — premium skins are pre-selected; every item is a checkbox, you control the layout.
  3. Download or share — save the image to your device, or create a link that shows only the items you picked and expires on its own.

Read-only by design

The tool can only display your collection. It cannot modify your account, client, gameplay, matchmaking, or ranking — and it gives no gameplay advantage.

Nothing stored

No credentials or tokens are ever persisted. A share link stores only the items you chose to show, never your account login, and the downloaded image contains no link.

Free & non-commercial

Free tier for everyone, no ads, no monetization. Built as a community tool for showing off collections.

Status

In development — public launch coming soon.
